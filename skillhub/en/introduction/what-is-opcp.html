The Private Gym Analogy

Imagine you want a gym for your employees. You have three options:

  • A public gym membership — affordable, but you share equipment with everyone. Crowded at peak hours, no control over the environment, and you have to follow someone else's rules.
  • Building your own gym from scratch — total control, but incredibly expensive. You buy the machines, hire maintenance staff, handle repairs, and manage everything yourself.
  • A fully managed gym installed in your own building — a professional company delivers the equipment to your premises, installs it, maintains it, and replaces anything that breaks. The gym is physically in your building, under your roof, but managed by experts.

OPCP is like that managed gym in your own building. OVHcloud delivers a rack of servers directly to your premises — your offices, your data room, your building. The hardware is physically at your location, but OVHcloud manages, monitors, and maintains it for you. You get the data locality and control of on-premise infrastructure without the operational burden.

What Does "On Premise Cloud Platform" Mean?

Let's break down the name into everyday language:

On Premise

The servers are physically located at your site — in your building, your server room, your premises. Unlike remote cloud services where your data lives in a distant data centre, with OPCP the hardware is right there with you. This gives you full physical proximity to your data and workloads.

Cloud

Even though the hardware is at your location, you manage it with modern cloud tools — a web dashboard, APIs, and automation. You can provision virtual machines, scale resources, and monitor performance just like you would with any cloud service.

Platform

It's a complete, ready-to-use infrastructure platform. OVHcloud delivers, installs, and maintains the rack of servers. You don't need to source hardware, hire specialists, or worry about firmware updates. It's a turnkey solution on your premises.

How OPCP Fits In

Key Takeaways

  • OPCP stands for On Premise Cloud Platform — a rack of servers installed at your premises and managed by OVHcloud.
  • It combines the data locality and control of on-premise infrastructure with the convenience of a fully managed cloud service.
  • You don't need technical expertise to understand or discuss OPCP — think of it as having a professional gym installed in your own building, maintained by experts.
  • Your data stays physically at your location, giving you full control over data sovereignty and compliance requirements.
