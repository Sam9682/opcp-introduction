What is On-Prem Cloud Platform (OPCP)?

OPCP is a comprehensive solution from OVHcloud for deploying, automating, and maintaining on-premises cloud computing environments. It brings the power of a flexible, innovative, and secure cloud directly where you need it: on-site, at the edge, or in isolated and critical environments.

As OVHcloud puts it: "It's not just a box, it's a full cloud platform."

Become a Sovereign Cloud Provider

Whether for your internal needs or for your clients, OPCP delivers a modern cloud environment straight to your datacentres. With a comprehensive stack, you can deploy, automate, and maintain your own on-premises cloud infrastructure, effectively becoming your own cloud provider.

Take the Cloud Where You Need It

OPCP lets you deploy a granular cloud infrastructure at each strategic site to process your data in real time, run critical applications without interruption, and ensure service continuity — even during network outages.

Operate in Disconnected Mode

OPCP provides organisations that require complete isolation with a cloud experience tailored to their compliance and sovereignty requirements. It can operate in a fully independent, disconnected mode.

Tackling Modern Business Challenges

OPCP is designed to address the needs of various industries:

  • Banking & Financial Sector — Compliance and sovereignty with data localisation, audit trails, and full flow control.
  • Manufacturing & Smart Factories — Ultra-low latency and reliability, close to the point of operation with zero tolerance for interruptions.
  • Sensitive Environments — Secure edge computing with an embedded, autonomous, and highly secure cloud.
  • Critical Activities & Service Continuity — High availability with redundant infrastructure to ensure business continuity.

Modular Three-Layer Architecture

OPCP is based on a modular architecture with three layers. Each layer has a specific role:

  1. OPCP Core — Industrial Infrastructure Automation

    OPCP Core fully automates infrastructure management (server discovery, network configuration, resource allocation) and ensures complete traceability, integrated security, and native observability. It is designed for large-scale industrial operations, leveraging orchestration tools and advanced automation.

  2. Cloud Store — Simplified Service Deployment & Maintenance

    Cloud Store lets you deploy pre-configured services in just a few clicks: virtualisation, containers, databases, AI, and business tools. Accessible from a local, automated, and expandable catalogue, it includes your in-house stacks as well as those of third-party providers. It gives you access to services such as Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, S3 storage, OVHcloud AI solutions, and more.

  3. Landing Zone Manager — Workload Deployment & Governance

    Landing Zone Manager provides development teams with a dedicated space to deploy, manage, and maintain their workloads. Each environment is isolated, traceable, and compliant — with fine-grained access, quota, and lifecycle management. It implements an on-premises cloud framework where workloads are protected and organised to your standards, based on cloud best practices.

Choose Your Delegation Level

Managed by you or managed for you: depending on your internal capabilities, priorities, and whether you need to work in a disconnected environment, you can adjust how much you delegate platform management and support.

In all cases, OVHcloud support covers the various layers, optionally extending to open-source applications available in the Cloud Store catalogue.
