Overview

OVHcloud Professional Services (PS) provides expert guidance throughout the lifecycle of an OPCP project. This lesson summarises the PS engagement model, delivery approach, project lifecycle activities, and key deliverables.

Value Proposition

OPCP's complexity requires a structured approach and deep expertise. Professional Services helps you meet business goals at every stage of transformation with services tailored to your unique needs:

  • Outcome-based services — focused on delivering measurable business value and maximising time-to-value from technology investments.
  • Expert consultants — dedicated OPCP Solution Architects with deep knowledge of the platform.
  • Best practices — leveraging application stack design experience and proven methodologies.

The primary mission is BUILD: guiding you through designing and implementing use cases on your sovereign cloud, accelerating the secure delivery of your live operational environment.

Engagement Model

Scope and Responsibilities

  • Primary focus is the Build phase of applications onto OPCP in a non-production environment.
  • Technical workshops for in-depth qualification.
  • Responsible for the Low-Level Architecture Design (LLD).
  • Cadenced execution of the LLD according to customer milestones.
  • Priming with OVHcloud partners (preferred) or side-by-side delivery.
  • Non-technical advisory services (People / Process / Tools) for better outcomes.

Engagement Framework

  • A Statement of Work (SoW) is mandatory before PS Solution Architects are engaged.
  • Outcome-based focus (value-oriented).
  • Deliverables approval required at each phase.

Delivery Approach

  • Project-managed delivery: planning, coordination, and reporting.
  • Scrum/Agile, waterfall, or hybrid methods as needed. A Scrum master should be identified on the customer side for cadenced execution.
  • Ongoing project tracking: health, activities, risks, and budget consumption.

OPCP Project Lifecycle Activities

Professional Services activities span the full project lifecycle, from initial goals through to business outcomes:

1. Discover and Assess

  • Review solution requirements and desired outcomes.
  • Technically qualify customer use cases onto OPCP.
  • Ensure stakeholder alignment and executive sponsorship.
  • Evaluate current state and readiness (People, Process, Tools).
  • Assist with operating model and target organisation.
  • Develop the Statement of Work (SoW).

2. Plan and Design

  • Conduct use-case-specific architecture design workshops.
  • Design the target detailed architecture (LLD) and develop a custom Configuration Workbook for deployment via CloudApps and OPCP Core.
  • Build the Implementation Plan and establish the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
  • Build and launch the training plan for new skills.

3. Build and Deploy (Deliver)

  • Ensure hardware delivery and OPCP installation with initial setup — up to "Ready for Use" state.
  • Integrate the platform with the customer environment and implement secured connectivity.
  • Configure OPCP using the Configuration Workbook.
  • Test and validate — up to "Ready for Service" state.
  • Provide As-Built documentation at the end of implementation.

4. Migrate and Handover

  • Provide a migration and test plan by defining lots of migration.
  • Migrate or implement application workloads.
  • Execute user acceptance testing.
  • Develop custom run playbooks / runbooks.
  • Handover to the operations team at go-live.

5. Train, Scale and Optimise

  • Ensure upskill at all required levels of the customer organisation.
  • On-site learning and OPCP certification for trainees.
  • Post go-live hypercare.
  • Continuous improvement.
  • Steady-state operations (operated by OVHcloud support centre).

Key Deliverables

Low-Level Design (LLD)

The LLD details the technical and functional architecture of the target solution, addressing both the "what" and "why" of the implementation. It covers scalability, functionality, integration, and operational requirements.

  • Developed through design workshops led by PS Solution Architects with deep OPCP expertise.
  • Involves close collaboration with customer stakeholders (architecture, virtualisation, networking teams).
  • Contains physical and logical diagrams, configuration templates, scripts, and artefacts.
  • Self-explanatory — enables non-specialists to perform most configurations based on the document.
  • Living document that evolves throughout the project lifecycle.

Implementation Plan

The Implementation Plan provides clear instructions, proven methodologies, and best practices for deploying and validating the OPCP platform tailored to solution requirements.

  • Built upon the HLD, LLD, and Configuration Workbook from the design phase.
  • Focuses on aligning OPCP configuration to customer use cases, workload integration, legacy connectivity, and automation/operations requirements.
  • Reduces unforeseen issues and standardises deployments through a reproducible framework.
  • Enables seamless handover to operations teams and integration into existing playbooks/runbooks.
  • Minimises misconfiguration risks and accelerates platform adoption.
