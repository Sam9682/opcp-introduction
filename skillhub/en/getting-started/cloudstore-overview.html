What is Cloud Store?

Cloud Store is the Platform as a Service (PaaS) layer of the OPCP stack. It lets you deploy cloud environments like apps: easily provision ready-to-use platforms and services — virtual machines, containers, databases, AI, and storage — for your users or clients, directly on your premises.

With Cloud Store, you get direct access to all of OVHcloud's software stack expertise on-site, with full control over your infrastructure.

Next-Generation Service Catalogue

Cloud Store gives you access to a wide selection of preconfigured cloud services:

  • Kubernetes — Container orchestration
  • PostgreSQL — Managed relational databases
  • S3 Storage — Object storage compatible with AWS SDKs
  • OVHcloud AI Solutions — Machine learning and inference
  • And more services from a growing catalogue

Each service is validated and maintained, so it can be quickly deployed, standardised, and managed with total control within your technical framework. You can also bundle your own services and applications to automate their deployment.

Native Integration with OPCP Core

Cloud Store is directly integrated with OPCP Core (the infrastructure layer) and automatically provides the hardware resources needed for each service: servers, storage, and networking.

The entire lifecycle of your services is managed to ensure a smooth, simple user experience, eliminating the need for complex configurations.

Multi-Tenancy and Fine-Grained Provisioning

Designed for complex environments, Cloud Store enables:

  • Detailed quota management — Allocate resources precisely by project, team, or client
  • Complete project isolation — Segment departments, clients, or environments securely
  • Usage monitoring — Track resource consumption in real time

Every resource is allocated with precision to maintain performance, security, and cost efficiency.

Key Benefits

Speed and Simplicity

Deploy services and applications quickly with built-in automation. Teams can reduce complexity, complete projects faster, and gain greater autonomy.

  • 1-click deployment — Automated provisioning, updates, backups, and recovery
  • Full application lifecycle — Large-scale patching and versioning
  • Faster innovation — New services available in minutes
  • Simplified reusability — Services packaged and reused to save time

Protection and Compliance

Cloud Store natively integrates the security and compliance mechanisms required for on-site environments. Every service comes ready to use and isolated, with access control and full traceability.

  • Multi-tenant isolation — Segmentation of projects, departments, or clients
  • Native IAM — Integrated SSO, RBAC, and MFA for precise access control
  • Audit and traceability — Centralised, transparent, and audit-ready logs
  • Easy compliance — A standards-compliant architecture for faster certification

Control and Transparency

Cloud Store provides oversight, metrics, and tracking for every service, giving you full control of your resources.

  • Native monitoring — Integrated logs, metrics, and alerts (Prometheus, Grafana)
  • Standardised governance — Unified access policies and quotas
  • Full transparency — Real-time tracking and reporting

Scalability and Modernisation

Cloud Store is designed to meet both current and future requirements.

  • Sustainability — Shared technological foundation with OVHcloud public services
  • Customisable catalogue — Bundle and deploy your own applications
  • Horizontal scaling — Scale without redesign
  • Open technology — Open-source foundations and OVHcloud ecosystem compatibility

Strategic Use Cases

Cloud Provider

Turn your infrastructure into a real service platform. Offer internal teams and clients access to Cloud Store's catalogue of managed applications (databases, VMs, Kubernetes, middleware), deployable in one click, with built-in billing and governance features.

Platform Engineering

Provide DevOps teams with a unified platform equipped with prebuilt CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes, and application services. Leverage Cloud Store to speed up standardisation, reduce technical debt, and build a sturdy base for large-scale platform engineering.

Cutting-Edge On-Site Technologies

Incorporate advanced technologies such as GPU-ready AI, sovereign data platforms, distributed databases, and analytics into your datacentre. Process confidential data locally without relying on an external provider.

Large-Scale Application Deployment

Package and deploy applications across multiple locations (branches, factories, retail stores). Cloud Store provides a unified system with fast deployment, regular updates, and total operational control across 200+ sites.

Cloud Store in Action

Cloud Store offers more than just a catalogue. It changes how teams utilise and manage cloud services through four key capabilities:

  • Click & Deploy service catalogue — Deploy databases, Kubernetes clusters, AI services, or storage solutions in seconds from a single catalogue
  • Integrated supervision and governance — Manage deployed services in real time with unified dashboards bringing together metrics, logs, and alerts
  • Quotas and multi-tenant management — Allocate quotas and resources by project, team, or client to ensure user autonomy with centralised control
  • Full lifecycle management — From automated provisioning to secure decommissioning, the entire process is orchestrated to accelerate product launches and strengthen governance

Key Takeaways

  • Cloud Store is the PaaS layer of OPCP — a service catalogue for deploying ready-to-use cloud platforms on your premises.
  • It provides access to Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, S3 storage, AI solutions, and more, all preconfigured and maintained.
  • It integrates natively with OPCP Core, which automatically provisions the required hardware resources.
  • Built-in multi-tenancy, IAM, monitoring, and compliance make it enterprise-ready.
  • It covers use cases from cloud provider platforms to large-scale edge deployments.
